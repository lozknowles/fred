All derivatives here retain per-asset provenance and source SHA256 in active assets.json and report.json.

Exact downloaded metadata is retained in blenderkit-source-metadata.json. Source blend remains in the user's local Blender library; export-blend.py deterministically evaluates one subdivision level, decimates dense parts, centers unit geometry, embeds 512px original maps. This is an affordable static derivative of the actual downloaded asset, including curved timber base, piping, woven cushions and pillows.

Run export-blend.py in Blender 4.5.14, then node integrate.mjs on the recorded baseSnapshot. The original IDs remain on retired visual records; the solid long-sofa and return bases are converted to collision-only boxes with their exact former world bounds. New decorative visuals have no collision, interaction, access or portal capability. Existing TV/terminal/doorway/structural data is untouched. Frame-rate and final visual acceptance require the parent task's native scene review.

Lantern base rests on side-table top y3.70.
