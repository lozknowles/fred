This download contains the Fred world engine, a pinned copy of Loz's authored island from Fred, and two stock player characters. The island includes reusable warehouse furniture prepared from the ElseMesh warehouse recipe. Its own repository and this release retain attribution, provenance, and asset licences.

Warehouse games room

The upright plays a 30-second excerpt from two Defender screen recordings supplied by Loz, with their recorded stereo sound. Defender is the Williams Electronics arcade game. Press E at the snooker table or the cabinet to play the excerpt. The speaker falls off with distance. You can mute it at the cabinet.

The snooker player demonstrates a double and a plant. Approach the table and press E to aim and play your own shot.

The upstairs machines use Tiny8bit, jsbeeb and a disposable NetBSD/SPARC 1.6.2 guest on QEMU. The C64 loads Snake by David Van Wagner. The Sun runs XFishtank on its own X display.
